# Thumbnail Recipe — "Zero Risk" USB Concept
## Tool: Canva (free, browser-based, works on Linux)

---

## Step 1 — Open Canva

1. Go to https://canva.com
2. Click **"Create a design"**
3. Search for **"YouTube Thumbnail"** (1280 x 720 px)
4. Wait — the template page loads

The free version is enough. Do NOT click "Canva Pro" features.

---

## Step 2 — Background

1. Click **"Background"** in the left panel
2. Search for **"dark gradient"**
3. Pick a **dark blue to black** gradient
   - Or click **"Photos"** → search **"dark blue abstract"** → pick a clean one
4. Click the image to add it to your canvas

**Why dark:** Makes text and the glowing USB pop. Newbies associate dark backgrounds with "tech" and "clean."

---

## Step 3 — The Old Laptop

1. Click **"Elements"** in the left panel
2. Search **"laptop"**
3. Scroll down to **"Illustrations"** section
4. Pick a **simple laptop icon** — flat style, grey or white
   - Avoid realistic photos (too busy)
   - Look for a simple, clean line-art laptop
5. Click it to add to canvas
6. Click and drag corners to resize — make it fill about 40% of the screen
7. Position it in the **center-bottom** of the canvas

---

## Step 4 — The Glowing USB

1. Click **"Elements"** → search **"USB drive"** or **"flash drive"**
2. Pick a simple **illustration of a USB stick**
   - If no good USB icon, search **"glowing light"** or **"sparkle"**
3. Size it smaller — about 1/4 of the laptop size
4. Position it **hovering above the laptop USB port**

**To make it glow:**
- Click the USB image
- Click **"Effects"** in the top toolbar
- Click **"Glow"** (free effect)
- Pick a **cyan/blue** glow color
- Adjust intensity slider to medium

---

## Step 5 — Add Sparkle / Light Rays

1. Click **"Elements"** → search **"sparkle"** or **"light rays"**
2. Pick a simple **star sparkle** or **light burst** graphic
3. Place it behind the USB
4. Reduce opacity to about **40%** using the slider in the top bar
5. Resize so it radiates outward

**Alternative:** Search **"magic light"** — pick a soft glowing circle, make it large, place it behind the USB, set opacity to 30%.

---

## Step 6 — BIG TEXT Overlay

1. Click **"Text"** in the left panel
2. Click **"Add a heading"**
3. Type: **ZERO RISK**
4. Highlight the text → In the top bar:
   - Font: **Impact** or **Montserrat Bold** (both free)
   - Size: **100-120px**
   - Color: **White**
5. Click **"Effects"** → **"Shadow"** → Pick a **thick black shadow**
   - This makes it readable over any background
6. Position it **top-left** or **top-center**

**Second line (smaller):**
1. Add another text box
2. Type: **JUST TRY IT**
3. Font: same as above
4. Size: **50-60px**
5. Color: **Cyan / Bright blue** (#00D2FF)
6. Add **black shadow** effect
7. Place directly below "ZERO RISK"

---

## Step 7 — Small Annotation (Optional)

1. Add a small text box
2. Type: **No install. No risk.**
3. Font size: **25-30px**
4. Color: **Light grey**
5. Position bottom-right corner
6. Needed? Not really. It fills space.

---

## Step 8 — Polish

Look at your canvas. Ask:

- Does the USB clearly look like it's going INTO the laptop? → If not, move it closer
- Is the text readable? → If not, make shadow thicker
- Is there too much clutter? → Remove anything that isn't laptop / USB / text
- Would a beginner understand this in 2 seconds? → If not, simplify

**Final check:** Squint your eyes. Can you still read "ZERO RISK"? Good.

---

## Step 9 — Download

1. Click **"Share"** (top-right)
2. Click **"Download"**
3. Format: **PNG**
4. Click **"Download"**
5. Save as: `ep2_thumbnail.png`

## Font & Color Rules (Keep This Consistent)

| Element | Setting |
|---|---|
| Main text font | Impact or Montserrat Bold |
| Main text color | White |
| Main text size | 100-120px |
| Sub text font | Same as main |
| Sub text color | Cyan #00D2FF |
| Sub text size | 50-60px |
| Shadow | Black, thick, on all text |
| Background | Dark gradient (blue→black) |
| Mood | Clean, simple, hopeful |

**One rule:** Never use more than 6 words on a thumbnail. Your viewers scroll past in 0.3 seconds.
