2 Requirements — Gate by Gate

GATE 0 — Every learner

    Must master typing before any class.
    Bar: 25+ WPM at 95% accuracy, 3 runs in a row.
    Tools: Klavaro / Tux Typing (Linux) or browser trainer.

No typing master → no enrolment. No exceptions.

TIER 1 — The doorway

    Succeed in the 10-episode Digital Independence course.
    Exit: quiz + script deliverable + public GitHub repo.
    Becomes your “Digital Independence certificate.”

No Tier 1 → no Track A and no Track B. This floor stays strict.

Policy (use as-is): “No learner may register for any course without first passing the Typing Gate (25 WPM @ 95%). All learners must hold the Digital Independence certificate before enrolling in either the Office or Geospatial tracks. QGIS Advanced, Python, and AI modules require completion of the preceding required module.”

A Track A — Office Productivity (LibreOffice)

1Calc (10 sessions) — spreadsheets, data, formulas
2Writer (6 sessions) — documents & reports
3Impress (4 sessions) — presentations

Order: data first — every report starts as a table. Careers: administration, data entry, NGO reporting, teaching materials.

Later move? Track A graduates may join Track B with the Tier 1 certificate. Calc is a bonus, not a requirement, for GIS.

B Track B — Geospatial Career (GIS & Automation)

1GIS & QGIS Beginners (10 weeks) — needs Tier 1. Coordinate systems, vector/raster, map layouts, data capture.
2QGIS Advanced (4 weeks) — needs the full beginner course. No mid-way entry.
3Python for GIS — needs QGIS Advanced. Calc thinking recommended for attribute tables.
4PyQGIS automation — needs Python for GIS. Batch geospatial processing.
5AI for geospatial analytics & dashboards — last stage; builds on PyQGIS output.
6Web mapping (Folium / Leaflet) — capstone: share interactive maps with decision makers.
Keep the branches open: both tracks exit into the same pool. A learner may apply Office skills in community/NGO work, or continue up the GIS/Python ladder. Certificates let learners choose later — no dead ends.
