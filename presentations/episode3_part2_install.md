Important warning: This will erase everything on your computer. If you have school projects, family photos, or important documents — back them up first. Copy them to an external drive or cloud storage before we begin.

This is the only time in this course where there is real risk. Pay attention, follow each step carefully, and you'll be fine."

---

## 3:00-7:00 — Booting from USB

### Visual
Boot from Ventoy USB. Wait for desktop. Locate the MX Installer icon.

### Script
"Boot from your Ventoy USB like we did last week. Plug it in, restart, tap the boot key, select MX Linux, wait for the desktop.

On the desktop, you'll see an icon that says 'MX Installer'. Double-click it.

The installer launches. It's completely graphical — no terminal, no commands. Just click through the screens.

First screen: click Next. Choose your language. Click Next. Select your time zone by clicking your location on the map. Click Next. Choose your keyboard layout — US is standard. Click Next."

---

## 7:00-14:00 — The Partition Screen

### Visual
Show the partitioning screen. Zoom in on "Use Entire Disk". Point at it clearly.

### Script
"Now we reach the screen that makes everyone nervous: disk partitioning.

I want you to look at the options. Find the one that says 'Use entire disk'. Select it.

This is the easy button. It tells the installer: 'Take this entire hard drive, erase everything, and set it up automatically.' You do not need to know about partitions, swap space, or file systems. The computer handles all of that.

Click Next. A warning appears: 'Are you sure? This will erase all data.' If you have backed up your files, click Yes.

This is the point of no return. After you click Yes, your old operating system is gone, and Linux is being installed."

---

## 14:00-18:00 — User Account

### Visual
Show the user creation form. Type in a name, username, password.

### Script
"The installer now asks for your information.

Your name: type your full name.
Username: choose one word, lowercase — like 'saltibin' or 'student'.
Password: choose something you will remember. This password protects your files and is needed every time you install software or make system changes.
Computer name: leave the default or pick something fun.

Click Next. Review the summary screen. If everything looks correct, click Install.

A progress bar appears. This takes 5 to 10 minutes depending on your hard drive speed. The installer is copying MX Linux onto your computer and setting everything up.

Do NOT power off or remove the USB during installation."

---

## 18:00-22:00 — First Boot

### Visual
Show installation complete screen. Remove USB. Restart. Boot into MX Linux from hard drive.

### Script
"When the installer says 'Installation complete', it will prompt you to restart. Click Finish.

The screen will tell you to remove the installation medium. Pull out your USB drive, then press Enter.

Your computer restarts. This time, it boots directly into MX Linux from your hard drive. The welcome screen appears. Enter your password. Look at your new permanent desktop.

You now have a complete Linux operating system installed and running. This is your computer now."
